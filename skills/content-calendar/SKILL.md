# Content calendar

## Pillars (3–5)
Each post belongs to one pillar. Typical B2B tech set (adapt to the brief or training notes):
1. **Expertise**: how something works, mistakes to avoid, checklists (e.g. GRC, compliance, AI governance).
2. **Product**: progress, features, demos, roadmap moments (only what is public).
3. **Team & culture**: behind the scenes, sprint results, people, hiring, events.
4. **Industry**: news, regulation changes, reports, with a short opinion and source.
5. **Community**: questions, polls, celebrating others, partners (with permission).

## Rhythm (starting point; change it when the team says)
| Platform | Posts / week | Best formats |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 2–3 | insight post, carousel, short article, team story |
| Instagram | 2–3 | carousel, single card, reel idea, story (polls, behind the scenes) |
| X | 3–5 | one idea, short thread, news reaction |
One topic can feed all three platforms with different angles; never paste the same text everywhere.

## Topic sources (real things only)
- The brief, the team's recent work (sprint reports, releases, documents in the workspace or project).
- Industry news from the last 30 days: cite URL and date; prefer official and primary sources.
- Questions customers or the community asked. Never invent numbers, customers, quotes or partnerships.

## Plan file: `social/plan.md`
```
# Social plan: <week, e.g. 10–16 Nov 2026>
Goal this week: <awareness / leads / hiring / community>
| # | Day | Pillar | Topic | Platforms | Angle and hook idea | Visual idea | Facts allowed (source) |
|---|-----|--------|-------|-----------|---------------------|-------------|------------------------|
## Notes for the Content Maker
- Tone, words to avoid, hashtags (1 branded + 2–4 topical), links.
## Open questions
- Facts or approvals still needed.
```
Check: every topic has a pillar and a source for its facts; at least one value post per platform; dates fall in the
requested week; nothing confidential (internal numbers, client names) without explicit permission.
